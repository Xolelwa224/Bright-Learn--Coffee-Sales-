# 📖 Project Overview

The **Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis** project demonstrates an end-to-end Business Intelligence workflow that transforms raw transactional data into actionable business insights.

Using SQL, Databricks, Microsoft Excel, Power Query, and interactive dashboards, this project analyzes six months of coffee shop sales data to identify revenue trends, customer purchasing behaviour, best-selling products, and operational opportunities.

The objective is to support executive decision-making by providing data-driven recommendations that improve profitability, optimize inventory management, and enhance customer experience.

---

# 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to analyze historical sales data and answer key business questions that help management improve overall business performance.

The analysis aims to:

* Increase revenue
* Identify top-performing products
* Understand customer purchasing behaviour
* Optimize staffing during peak trading periods
* Improve inventory planning
* Support strategic business decision-making

---

# ❓ Business Questions

This analysis answers the following business questions:

* Which products generate the highest revenue?
* Which products sell the highest quantities?
* Which product categories perform best?
* What are the busiest and slowest trading hours?
* How do monthly sales trends change over time?
* Which products require improvement?
* What opportunities exist to increase revenue?

# 📈 Executive Insight Summary

## Overall Business Performance

* The coffee shop maintained consistent sales performance throughout the six-month period.
* Revenue remained relatively stable, indicating healthy customer demand and effective day-to-day operations.
* Coffee products consistently generated the largest share of revenue.

---

## Product Performance

### Highest Revenue Products

* Coffee generated the highest overall revenue.
* Bakery products were among the strongest-performing complementary purchases.
* Tea maintained steady sales but contributed less revenue than coffee.

### Lowest Revenue Products

* Packaged Chocolate
* Loose Tea
* Flavours

These products consistently recorded the lowest sales and revenue throughout the analysis period.

---

## Quantity Sold Analysis

* Coffee was the most purchased product across every month.
* Bakery items showed consistently high sales volumes.
* Packaged Chocolate remained the least purchased product.

---

## Monthly Sales Trends

Sales remained relatively stable across the six-month period, with only minor fluctuations.

The consistency suggests:

* Strong customer retention
* Stable demand
* Effective operational management

---

## Customer Purchasing Behaviour

Analysis shows customers primarily purchase:

* Coffee
* Bakery Items

Customers frequently purchase bakery products alongside beverages, presenting opportunities for bundled promotions and upselling strategies.

---

## Peak Trading Hours

The busiest trading periods occurred during morning and early afternoon hours, indicating customers primarily visit before work and during lunch breaks.

These hours contribute significantly to total daily revenue.

---

## Business Opportunities

The analysis identified several opportunities to increase revenue:

* Bundle low-performing products with best sellers.
* Promote underperforming products during quieter periods.
* Expand the coffee product range.
* Introduce seasonal beverages.
* Reward repeat customers through loyalty programs.

---

# 💼 Business Recommendations

### Increase Inventory

Maintain sufficient stock levels for high-demand products, particularly coffee and bakery items, to prevent stock shortages during peak trading periods.

### Improve Product Visibility

Increase the visibility of low-performing products through strategic shelf placement and point-of-sale promotions.

### Product Bundling

Bundle slow-selling products with popular coffee items to encourage cross-selling and increase average transaction value.

### Peak Hour Staffing

Schedule additional staff during busy trading periods to reduce customer waiting times and improve service quality.

### Loyalty Programme

Implement a customer rewards programme to increase repeat purchases and strengthen customer retention.

### Promotional Campaigns

Run targeted promotions during slower trading hours to stimulate customer traffic and increase revenue.

### Expand Product Range

Introduce new seasonal beverages and bakery products based on customer purchasing behaviour.

### Automated Reporting

Develop automated dashboards for real-time monitoring of sales performance and faster executive decision-making.
